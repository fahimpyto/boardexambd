Frequently asked questions

Short answers based on the dates and instructions in this guide.

When do university admission applications open for 2026-27? +

The dates differ by university. DU applications run from 11 to 25 November 2026, RU opens on 12 November 2026, and BUET applications are expected from mid-November to early December 2026. Each university's official circular is final.

Which admission tests are coming next? +

The medical admission test is tentatively set for 4 December 2026, DU unit tests run from 5 to 26 December 2026, BUET's preliminary test is on 16 January 2027, and RU unit tests are planned for 8, 9 and 16 January 2027.

Where should I verify admission dates? +

Always verify with the official university or cluster portal — for example admission.eis.du.ac.bd, buet.ac.bd, dgme.teletalk.com.bd, gstadmission.ac.bd or admission.ru.ac.bd. BoardExamBD summarises the notices but is not an official source.
