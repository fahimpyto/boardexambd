What we know so far (real-time status)

As of 23 October 2026, the SUST admission circular for the 2026-27 session has not been published on the university's admission portal. Last year's schedule gives a strong pattern: applications closed on 10 November 2025 and the entry test for both units was held on Sunday, 7 December 2025. If SUST follows the same rhythm this year, the circular should appear around late October to November 2026 with the test in December 2026.

  • ✓ SUST admits through "A" unit (science & engineering) and "B" unit (other disciplines) — separate tests, separate merit lists.
  • ✓ Beyond the common GPA rule, departments can carry additional criteria — such as a minimum GPA of 3.0 in individual requirements — as listed in the prospectus.
  • ✓ Tests are held at SUST and other designated centres outside Sylhet — your admit card names your centre.

How to apply — step by step

  1. 1 Read the prospectus on the portal — it lists unit-wise departments, seats and the additional criteria (minimum GPA 3.0-type requirements) each department enforces.
  2. 2 Apply online in your unit (A or B) with SSC/HSC details, photo, signature and the application fee.
  3. 3 Download the admit card and note your unit test time and centre.
  4. 4 Sit the MCQ test — bring the signed admit card and original marksheet copies as instructed for verification at admission.
  5. 5 Check the merit list and complete admission within the enrollment window to keep your seat.
