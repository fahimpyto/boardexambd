What we know so far (real-time status)

The HSC 2026 form fill-up is already complete: students filled their online forms between 1 and 9 March 2026, with the fee submission deadline on 10 March 2026 and a late-fee extension running to about 18 March. Those candidates then sat the exams from 2 July, and their results are scheduled for 29 October 2026.

  • ✓ For the 2027 session, no circular has been issued yet as of 23 October 2026 — based on the last cycle, expect the fill-up window around February–March 2027.
  • ✓ Form fill-up runs through your college — the institution submits data to the board system; you confirm subjects and pay the fee at the college.
  • ✓ Media reported that form fill-up fees rose in the last cycle — fee changes are announced in each board's circular.
  • ✓ Late-fee windows exist after the normal deadline — but corrections get harder and costlier, so finish inside the regular window.

How it works — step by step

  1. 1 Confirm your subjects with the college — group papers, optional subject, and practical-based science subjects must match your registration and class records.
  2. 2 The college uploads candidate data to the board's online form fill-up system inside the announced window.
  3. 3 Pay the exam fee at the college by the fee-submission deadline — amounts come from the board's circular.
  4. 4 Verify everything on the printed copy — name spelling, roll/registration, subjects and photo — before the college finalizes it.
  5. 5 Keep the receipt safe — those details are needed for the admit card and, later, for checking your result and certificates.

The 2027 dates are expectations drawn from the 2026 cycle — the general boards plus the Madrasah (Alim) and Technical boards publish their own notices. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site — your college's notice and the board website are final.
