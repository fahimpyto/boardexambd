What we know so far (real-time status)#

Good news — the boards have already announced the form fill-up schedule for the SSC and equivalent examinations of 2027. The online form fill-up begins on Monday, 16 November 2026, and candidates can complete it without any late fee until 23 November 2026, according to the education boards' announcement covered in the media.

  • ✓ Form fill-up is not done by students directly — your school submits the data to the board system; you confirm your subjects and pay the fee at the institution.
  • ✓ The boards have published key instructions for candidates — accuracy in subject selection (including optional and practical subjects) matters most.
  • ✓ A late-fee window historically follows the no-fee window — don't count on it. Finish within 16–23 November.
  • ✓ Last cycle (2026 session), the process wrapped up well before the February 2026 exams, and those candidates received results on 10 August 2026.

Confirmed: 16–23 November 2026 without late fee. Fee amounts and the late-fee dates come with each board's notice — keep an eye on your school's office notice board in mid-November.

How it works — step by step#

  1. 1 Confirm your subject list with your class teacher — group subjects, optional (4th) subject and the practical-based science subjects must match your class records exactly.
  2. 2 Your school uploads the candidate data to the education board's online form fill-up system during the 16–23 November window.
  3. 3 Pay the exam fee at the institution as instructed — the fee schedule is set by each board's notice.
  4. 4 Check and sign the printed form — verify your name spelling, date of birth, subject codes and photo before the school locks the data.
  5. 5 Keep the money receipt safely — you'll need the registration details later to collect your admit card and, after the exams, your results.

Always confirm officially: fee amounts, late-fee windows and instruction lists are issued board by board (Dhaka, Rajshahi, Cumilla, etc. plus Madrasah & Technical). Your school's notice and the board website are final. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site.

Quick checklist

  • ✓ Pay the board-set fee through your institution and keep the receipt.
  • ✓ Check the printed form for name spelling, date of birth and photo.
  • ✓ Ask your school about the late-fee window only if you miss the main dates.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers based on the dates and instructions in this guide.

When is SSC form fill-up 2027? +

The announced online window is 16–23 November 2026 without a late fee. The exact fee and late-fee dates are set in each board's notice.

Do students fill up the SSC 2027 form themselves? +

No. The school uploads candidate data to the board's online system. Students confirm their subjects, pay the fee and verify the printed form at the institution.

What is the SSC 2027 form fill-up fee? +

The fee is set by the education boards and paid through your school. Check your school's notice or the board circular for the exact amount.

What happens if I miss the 23 November deadline? +

A late-fee window usually follows the main window, but it is harder and more expensive. Do not rely on it — finish the form fill-up within 16–23 November 2026.
