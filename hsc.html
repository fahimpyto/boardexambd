Frequently asked questions

Short answers based on the dates and instructions in this guide.

When did the HSC 2026 exams start? +

The HSC, Alim and equivalent written exams began on 2 July 2026 with the Bangla 1st paper. The written part ended on 8 August 2026, and practical exams were completed by 15 August 2026.

When is the HSC result 2026? +

The HSC, Alim and DIBS results are scheduled for 29 October 2026, according to the education boards' latest notice. Check the official board portals or our HSC result guide for the exact steps.

When is HSC form fill-up 2027? +

No 2027 circular had been issued as of 23 October 2026. Based on the 2026 cycle, the next form fill-up window is expected around February to March 2027 and will run through each college.
