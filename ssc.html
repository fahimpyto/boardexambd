Frequently asked questions

Short answers based on the dates and instructions in this guide.

When is the SSC 2026 exam? +

The SSC 2026 written exams began on 11 April 2026 with the Bangla 1st paper. Every exam started at 10:00 AM, and the boards' full subject-wise schedule is in our SSC 2026 routine guide.

When was the SSC result 2026 published? +

The SSC, Dakhil and Vocational results for 2026 were published on 10 August 2026 at 10:00 AM. Students could check the full marksheet online, by SMS or at their school.

When is SSC form fill-up 2027? +

The boards announced SSC 2027 form fill-up from 16 to 23 November 2026 without a late fee. The process runs through each school, which submits candidate data to the board.
