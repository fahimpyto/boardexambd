What we know so far (real-time status)

Rajshahi University has laid out its 2026-27 undergraduate admission schedule: preliminary applications start 12 November 2026, and the unit-wise admission tests are initially planned as Unit B on 8 January, Unit C on 9 January and Unit A on 16 January 2027 — with C and A units covering the OBC-type groups and science candidates respectively on the university's announcement.

  • ✓ The exam dates are the initially announced schedule — universities sometimes fine-tune them, so re-check the notices before your test.
  • ✓ Application, admit card and results all run through the admission portal — notices are also posted under Unit-A/Unit-B/Unit-C sections.
  • ✓ Last cycle (2025-26), the unit tests were held across 16, 17 and 24 January 2026 — a similar mid-January pattern this time.

How to apply — step by step

  1. 1 Match yourself to a unit — RU admits through A, B and C units aligned to your HSC group. Read the unit notices on the portal for each unit's departments.
  2. 2 Apply from 12 November 2026 at admission.ru.ac.bd — SSC/HSC roll and registration, photo, signature and fee payment per unit.
  3. 3 Download your admit card when the portal opens it, and check your building/room assignment at the Rajshahi campus.
  4. 4 Sit your unit test on the announced day — Unit B (8 Jan) and Unit C (9 Jan) run back-to-back, so students sitting both should plan travel accordingly.
  5. 5 Track results and admission notices under your unit's notice board on the portal — the 2025-26 cycle shows how migration and seat-confirmation notices follow the merit list round by round.
