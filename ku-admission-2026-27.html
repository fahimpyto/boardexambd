What we know so far (real-time status)#

Khulna University started its 2026-27 undergraduate admission process earlier than most universities: candidates were asked to submit their application forms online from 1 to 30 September 2026, confirmed by the university's acting director (media & public relations) — so as of 23 October 2026 the application window has already closed.

  • ✓ If you applied — the next step is the admit card and test schedule, announced unit-wise on the university's admission pages at ku.ac.bd.
  • ✓ KU holds discipline/school-wise admission tests — different disciplines test on different announced days rather than one common paper.
  • ✓ Last cycle (2025-26 session), the admission tests were held on 18–19 December 2025 — a reasonable anchor for how this cycle may roll, but the announced schedule is final.
  • ✓ Competition is heavy — in a recent session, media coverage reported roughly 97 candidates vying for each seat.

What happens next?#

  1. 1 Watch for the test schedule — KU publishes unit/discipline-wise dates, times and venues on its admission website. Screenshot your discipline's slot as soon as it appears.
  2. 2 Download your admit card within the announced window and print it — entry to the test hall requires the printed card.
  3. 3 Sit your discipline's test on the announced day with the admit card and a photo ID.
  4. 4 Check merit lists per discipline and complete the admission formalities within the given deadline to confirm your seat.

Missed the window? The application period (1–30 September 2026) has ended — unilateral late applications are not accepted. Only sources at ku.ac.bd can confirm schedule changes or supplementary notices. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers based on the dates and instructions in this guide.

When was the KU admission 2026-27 application? +

Khulna University accepted online applications from 1 to 30 September 2026. The window has now closed.

When is the KU admission test 2026-27? +

KU announces discipline-wise test dates on ku.ac.bd. The schedule had not been published as of 23 October 2026.

When were the KU admission tests last cycle? +

The 2025-26 admission tests were held on 18–19 December 2025. This cycle's official schedule may differ.

Can I still apply for KU admission 2026-27? +

No. The application window closed on 30 September 2026, and late applications are not accepted. Watch ku.ac.bd for any official supplementary notice.
