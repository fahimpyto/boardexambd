SSC Result 2026 — here is the answer#

Waiting over: the SSC and equivalent examination results for 2026 were published on 10 August 2026. The results went live on the board portals at 10:00 AM that morning. Students of the SSC exam, the Madrasah board's Dakhil exam and the Technical board's Vocational exam all got their results on the same day.

The boards had initially announced 8 August 2026 as the provisional date; the official publication was finally held on Monday, 10 August 2026.

Which boards' results are included?#

The SSC examination in Bangladesh runs across 11 education boards — nine general boards, the Madrasah board (which conducts the SSC-equivalent Dakhil exam) and the Technical board (which conducts the Vocational exam). The Bangladesh Open University also holds SSC-equivalent public examinations separately.

How the result is published#

The result does not appear suddenly — it follows a fixed process set by the Ministry of Education and the education boards:

  1. 1 Each board finishes evaluating the exam papers and checks them twice, then uploads the final results to its own system and sends a copy to the Ministry of Education.
  2. 2 The Ministry stores the results centrally on educationboardresults.gov.bd and announces the publication date through newspapers and TV.
  3. 3 On the publication day, the results are officially handed over to the Prime Minister / the government at the result handover ceremony (10:00 AM).
  4. 4 After that, the results go live for everyone — online, by SMS, and at the schools — so every student can get marks the same day.

How to check your SSC result — 3 easy ways#

Way 1

Online — full marksheet (recommended)#

  1. 1 · Open educationboardresults.gov.bd — the official board result portal.
  2. 2 · Select examination: SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent.
  3. 3 · Select year: 2026, then select your board.
  4. 4 · Type your Exam Roll number and Registration number.
  5. 5 · Fill in the security question and press Submit — your subject-wise marksheet opens, ready to save or print.
Way 2

SMS — works from any phone#

Write a message in this format and send it to 16222 or 16140 (any mobile operator):

SSC <space> BOARD <space> ROLL <space> 2026 → 16222

Example: SSC DHA 113456 2026 — board name as first 3 letters. Standard SMS charges apply.

Way 3

From your school#

Your institution also receives the results: simply visit your school office and collect your result/marksheet there — useful if the website is busy on result day.

Keep this in mind: open the portals early or late afternoon on result day — they get very busy right after 10:00 AM. If a number or website fails, try the SMS method. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site — always double-check with your education board's official sources.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers based on the dates and instructions in this guide.

When was the SSC result 2026 published? +

The SSC, Dakhil and Vocational results for 2026 were published on 10 August 2026 at 10:00 AM.

How can I check my SSC result 2026 online? +

Open educationboardresults.gov.bd, select SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent, choose 2026 and your board, then enter your exam roll and registration number.

What is the SMS format for SSC result 2026? +

Send SSC <space> BOARD <space> ROLL <space> 2026 to 16222 or 16140. For example: SSC DHA 113456 2026.

Which exams are covered by the SSC 2026 result? +

The result covers SSC, Dakhil and Vocational candidates across 11 education boards, including the nine general boards, the Madrasah board and the Technical board.
