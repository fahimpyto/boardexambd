## How the GST cluster works — step by step

1. 1
   **Check the guideline** — last cycle's guideline noted a minimum GPA of **3.50 in both SSC and HSC** (including optional subjects). The 2026-27 circular is the final word on eligibility.
2. 2
   **Apply once online** at gstadmission.ac.bd in your unit — science students apply to the **"A" unit** — and pay the cluster application fee.
3. 3
   **Download your admit card** before the test day and check your centre.
4. 4
   **Sit the one-hour MCQ test** — a single question paper is used for the whole cluster on your unit's day.
5. 5
   **Check your merit position** and fill your **university/department choice list** on the portal; admission is finalized round by round.
