## How BUET admission works — step by step

1. 1
   **Apply online** on the BUET admission site when the circular opens — fill your SSC and HSC details, photo and signature, and pay the application fee (Tk 1000 in the last cycle).
2. 2
   BUET prepares a **shortlist** from the applicant pool based on the selection criteria in the prospectus — admission is far more competitive than the number of seats.
3. 3
   Shortlisted candidates sit the **preliminary MCQ test** (16 January 2027).
4. 4
   Candidates advancing further take the **written test** a few weeks later — final merit is drawn from it.
5. 5
   Merit-list candidates **choose departments and confirm seat** on the portal with document verification.
