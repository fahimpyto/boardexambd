# HSC Result 2026 — Date & How to Check Online/SMS

> Result date for HSC, Alim and technical equivalent exams — plus the exact steps to get your full marksheet.

## Key facts

- **Result date:** 31 October 2026 — Thursday — scheduled
- **Online portals:** 2 ways — Board portal & eboardresults
- **Result by SMS:** 16222 — HSC <Board> <Roll> 2026
- **Covers:** HSC · Alim · DIBS — All boards, same day

## HSC Result 2026 — when is it?

The results of the **HSC, Alim and equivalent examinations of 2026 are scheduled to be published on Thursday, 31 October 2026** — according to the education boards' latest notice. The 2026 exams began on 2 July and the last written paper was held on 8 August 2026, with practical exams completed by 15 August.

- ✓ Results for **all 11 boards** come out together on the same day.
- ✓ Admission results usually go live **around 10:00 AM** in the morning.
- ✓ With your result you can see **GPA and the full marksheet** with subject marks.
- ✓ If you are not happy with any marks, there is a **re-scrutiny (board challenge)** window right after the result day — fees and forms are published by each board.

## How to check your result — 3 easy ways

Way 1

### Online — full marksheet (recommended)

1. 1 · Open [educationboardresults.gov.bd](https://www.educationboardresults.gov.bd) or [eboardresults.com](https://eboardresults.com).
2. 2 · Select examination: **HSC/Alim/Equivalent**.
3. 3 · Select year: **2026**, then select your **board**.
4. 4 · Type your **Exam Roll number** and **Registration number**.
5. 5 · Press **Submit** — your full marksheet opens, ready to save or print. This marksheet is accepted for university admission.

Way 2

### SMS — works from any phone

Send a message in the format below to **16222** (any mobile operator, works even without internet):

HSC <space> BOARD <space> ROLL <space> 2026 → 16222

Example: HSC DHA 113456 2026 — board name as first **3 letters**. Standard SMS charges apply.

Way 3

### From your college

Your college also receives the results. Visit the office after the result goes live and collect a printed copy — useful if the websites are busy on result day.

**Keep this in mind:** the result date above is from the boards' latest notice — final confirmation comes from your board on result day. Websites get busy shortly after 10:00 AM, so try again or use SMS. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site — always double-check with your education board's official sources.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the HSC result 2026?

The HSC, Alim and DIBS results are published on Saturday, 31 October 2026 (revised from 29 Oct), according to the education boards' latest notice.

### How can I check the HSC result 2026 online?

Open educationboardresults.gov.bd or eboardresults.com, select HSC/Alim/Equivalent, choose 2026 and your board, then enter your roll and registration number.

### What is the SMS format for the HSC result 2026?

Send HSC <space> BOARD <space> ROLL <space> 2026 to 16222. For example: HSC DHA 113456 2026.

### Which exams are covered by the HSC 2026 result?

The result covers HSC, Alim and DIBS candidates across all 11 education boards on the same day.
