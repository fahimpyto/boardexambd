# HSC Practical Exams 2026 — Schedule, Rules & Marks

> How the lab-based practical (and oral/viva) exams for the 2026 HSC ran — institutions, timing, rules and what happens for the next batch.

## Key facts

- **Practicals finished:** By 15 Aug 2026 — Done — all boards
- **Held at:** Own institution — Dates set by each college
- **Who takes them:** Science + ICT — Lab & viva-based papers
- **Written exams ran:** 2 Jul – 8 Aug 2026 — Done — written part over first

## What happened in 2026 (real-time recap)

The **HSC 2026 written exams** ran from 2 July to 8 August 2026, and after the written papers finished, the boards required all **practical examinations to be completed by 15 August 2026**. Unlike the written tests, practicals were **organized by each institution at its own centre** — every college announced its own date list and the boards assigned examiners.

- ✓ Practical exams cover the **science group** — Physics, Chemistry, Biology — plus papers with practical/oral parts such as ICT.
- ✓ Each institution scheduled its practical days **shift-wise** and announced them on its own notice board — there is no single board routine for practical dates.
- ✓ **Practical marks count inside the subject's total** — they merge with your theory marks in the final result.
- ✓ Alim (Madrasah) and BM/DIBS (Technical) candidates follow their own boards' equivalent practical arrangements.

## How practical exams work

1. 1
   **Your college announces the dates** — usually subject-wise and shift-wise, right after the written exams of that subject end.
2. 2
   **Bring your practical notebook and lab records** — examiners check them along with your experiment performance.
3. 3
   Board-appointed **external examiners** evaluate the experiment, viva/answer sheet and your notebook during the session.
4. 4
   The college consolidates **practical marks with the theory marks** and forwards them to the board for the final result.

## Quick checklist

- Check your college notice board for your subject-wise practical dates.
- Carry your practical notebook and lab records.
- Bring your admit card and a photo ID to the practical centre.
- Follow lab safety and examiner instructions during the session.
- Confirm that your practical marks are forwarded with the theory marks.

## Frequently asked questions

### When were the HSC 2026 practical exams held?

The HSC 2026 practical exams were completed by 15 August 2026, after the written exams ended on 8 August.

### Where were the HSC 2026 practical exams held?

Practicals were held at each student's own institution or assigned centre. Every college announced its own subject-wise and shift-wise dates.

### Who takes the HSC practical exams?

Science group students take practicals in subjects such as Physics, Chemistry and Biology, along with practical or oral papers such as ICT. Alim and BM/DIBS candidates follow their own board arrangements.

### Do HSC practical marks count in the final result?

Yes. Practical marks are merged with the theory marks for the subject and forwarded to the board for the final result.
