## How to apply — step by step

1. 1
   **Read the prospectus** on the portal — it lists unit-wise departments, seats and the additional criteria (minimum GPA 3.0-type requirements) each department enforces.
2. 2
   **Apply online** in your unit (A or B) with SSC/HSC details, photo, signature and the application fee.
3. 3
   **Download the admit card** and note your unit test time and centre.
4. 4
   **Sit the MCQ test** — bring the signed admit card and original marksheet copies as instructed for verification at admission.
5. 5
   **Check the merit list** and complete admission within the enrollment window to keep your seat.
