# SSC Board Challenge 2026 — Process, Fees & Result

> The full story of this year's re-scrutiny window — how students applied by SMS, the fees, and when the challenge results came out.

**Updated:** 2026-10-23 · **Published:** 2026-10-23 · **Author:** BoardExamBD Editorial Team · **Read time:** 3 min

[Read the formatted guide](https://boardexambd.com/ssc-board-challenge-2026)

## Key facts

- **Application window:** 11–17 Aug 2026 — Done — by SMS
- **Fee:** Tk 150 / paper — Two-paper subjects charged per paper
- **Challenge result:** 10 Sep 2026 — Published online
- **Applies from SMS:** 16222 — Any operator, pre-paid number

## What happened in 2026 (full recap)

Right after the **SSC 2026 results came out on 10 August 2026**, the education boards opened the **re-scrutiny (board challenge)** application window — it ran from **11 to 17 August 2026**. Students who doubted any marks applied by **SMS from their own mobile number**, paying **Tk 150 for each paper** challenged; for subjects with two papers, the fee is charged per paper. The challenge results were published on **10 September 2026**.

- ✓ Re-scrutiny only **re-checks the answer scripts** — whether marks were missed or added wrongly. It is not a re-examination.
- ✓ In the usual rule, the **fee is refunded when marks go up** after the challenge — details sit in each board's notice.
- ✓ No mark ever **goes down** in re-scrutiny under the standard board rules.
- ✓ Next cycle: the **SSC 2027** challenge window is expected right after the 2027 result — the same SMS flow will apply.

## How the SMS application worked

1. 1
   **First SMS** — from your own pre-paid number, send RSC <BOARD> <ROLL> <YEAR> — for example RSC DHA 123456 2026 — to **16222**.
2. 2
   The reply message shows your details and a **PIN**, plus how much fee will be charged for the papers you selected.
3. 3
   **Confirm** — send RSC <space> YES <space> PIN <space> CONTACT NUMBER to **16222** from a balance-ready number.
4. 4
   The **fee is deducted from mobile balance** — keep enough balance before confirming, and save the confirmation SMS.
5. 5
   Check the **challenge result** online on the board's result portal when it is published (this year: 10 September 2026).

## Quick checklist

- Check your full marksheet before deciding to challenge a paper.
- Apply only if you believe a mark was missed or added incorrectly.
- Use your own pre-paid mobile number for the SMS application.
- Send the RSC format to 16222 and confirm the PIN when prompted.
- Save the confirmation SMS and check the challenge result online.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the SSC board challenge?

The board challenge, or re-scrutiny, is a review of your answer scripts to check whether marks were missed or added incorrectly. It is not a re-examination.

### When was the SSC board challenge 2026 window?

The 2026 re-scrutiny application window ran from 11 to 17 August 2026 and was completed by SMS.

### How much did the SSC board challenge cost?

The fee was Tk 150 per paper. For subjects with two papers, the fee was charged for each paper, and the standard rule refunds the fee if marks increase.

### When was the SSC board challenge result published?

The 2026 challenge results were published on 10 September 2026. The next cycle is expected after the SSC 2027 result.
