## SSC Result 2026 — here is the answer

Waiting over: the **SSC and equivalent examination results for 2026 were published on **10 August 2026****. The results went live on the board portals at **10:00 AM** that morning. Students of the SSC exam, the Madrasah board's **Dakhil** exam and the Technical board's **Vocational** exam all got their results on the same day.

The boards had initially announced **8 August 2026** as the provisional date; the official publication was finally held on Monday, 10 August 2026.

## How the result is published

The result does not appear suddenly — it follows a fixed process set by the Ministry of Education and the education boards:

1. 1
   Each board finishes evaluating the exam papers and checks them twice, then **uploads the final results** to its own system and sends a copy to the Ministry of Education.
2. 2
   The Ministry stores the results centrally on **educationboardresults.gov.bd** and announces the publication date through newspapers and TV.
3. 3
   On the publication day, the results are **officially handed over to the Prime Minister / the government** at the result handover ceremony (10:00 AM).
4. 4
   After that, the results go live for everyone — **online, by SMS, and at the schools** — so every student can get marks the same day.

## How to check your SSC result — 3 easy ways

Way 1

### Online — full marksheet (recommended)

1. 1 · Open [educationboardresults.gov.bd](https://www.educationboardresults.gov.bd) — the official board result portal.
2. 2 · Select examination: **SSC/Dakhil/Equivalent**.
3. 3 · Select year: **2026**, then select your **board**.
4. 4 · Type your **Exam Roll number** and **Registration number**.
5. 5 · Fill in the security question and press **Submit** — your subject-wise marksheet opens, ready to save or print.

Way 2

### SMS — works from any phone

Write a message in this format and send it to **16222** or **16140** (any mobile operator):

SSC <space> BOARD <space> ROLL <space> 2026 → 16222

Example: SSC DHA 113456 2026 — board name as first **3 letters**. Standard SMS charges apply.

Way 3

### From your school

Your institution also receives the results: simply visit your school office and collect your result/marksheet there — useful if the website is busy on result day.

**Keep this in mind:** open the portals early or late afternoon on result day — they get very busy right after 10:00 AM. If a number or website fails, try the SMS method. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site — always double-check with your education board's official sources.
