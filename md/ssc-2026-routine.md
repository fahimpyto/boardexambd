## What the notice says

The Boards of Intermediate & Secondary Education — **Dhaka, Rajshahi, Cumilla, Jashore, Barishal, Sylhet, Dinajpur, Chattogram and Mymensingh** — have published the routine for the **School Certificate (SSC) Examination 2026** (Notice ref. প্রশা/২০২৬/০৮, dated ১৫/০১/২০২৬).

In simple words:

- ✓ Every exam will run **as per the schedule published** — subject by subject, day by day.
- ✓ **Each exam starts at 10:00 AM (সকাল ১০:০০টা)** — be seated well before that.
- ✓ The boards can **change the routine if needed** — so keep checking for updates.

## Important rules from the notice

The “special instructions” (বিশেষ নির্দেশনাবলি) on page 2, in plain language:

- ✓
  Be seated in the exam hall **at least 30 minutes before** the exam starts — i.e. by 9:30 AM. Exams begin exactly at **10:00 AM**.
- ✓
  The **MCQ part is held first**, followed by the creative/written part — with **no break in between**.
- ✓
  Collect your **admit card from your institution head at least 3 days before** the exam date. No admit card — no entry.
- ✓
  On the **OMR sheet**, fill your roll number, registration number, subject code, etc. correctly — mark the circles properly. **White/correction fluid is not allowed**.
- ✓
  Only **board-approved calculators** are allowed in the exam hall.
- ✓
  **Mobile phones are strictly prohibited** inside the exam hall — never bring one in.
- ✓
  Candidates use **one common attendance sheet** for both the creative (theory) and practical exams.
- ✓
  Music, science and other **practical/viva papers follow the separate practical schedule** on page 2 — check that when it applies to your subject.

**Keep this in mind:** the notice itself says the board authority **can change the routine if needed**. Before each exam, double-check the date and subject with your institution or your own board's official notice. ## Quick checklist

- Confirm your subject-wise dates on the official board routine.
- Reach the exam centre by 9:30 AM — every paper starts at 10:00 AM.
- Carry your admit card and registration card to every exam.
- Leave mobile phones and unauthorised calculators outside the hall.
- Re-check the board notice before each exam in case the routine changes.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does the SSC 2026 exam start?

The SSC 2026 exams begin on 11 April 2026 with the Bangla 1st paper. Every paper starts at 10:00 AM.

### What time do SSC 2026 exams start?

All SSC 2026 exams start at 10:00 AM. Students should be seated in the exam hall by 9:30 AM.

### Which boards follow this SSC routine?

This routine applies to the nine general education boards — Dhaka, Rajshahi, Cumilla, Jashore, Barishal, Sylhet, Dinajpur, Chattogram and Mymensingh. Madrasah and Technical board candidates follow their own notices.

### Can the SSC 2026 routine change?

Yes. The education boards can change the routine if needed, so check your board's official notice before each exam.
