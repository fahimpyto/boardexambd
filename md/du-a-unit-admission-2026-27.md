## What we know so far (real-time status)

As of 23 October 2026, Dhaka University has confirmed its **2026-27 undergraduate admission schedule**: online applications open at **12:00 noon on 11 November 2026** and close at **11:59 PM on 25 November 2026**. The **exact day of the "A" unit (science) test** inside that window is confirmed on the admission portal with the seat plan — based on previous cycles, science-unit candidates should expect the test around mid-December. Keep the portal bookmarked so you don't miss the announcement.

## How to apply — step by step

1. 1
   **Check eligibility** — for the A (science) unit the common requirement is a combined **GPA of 8.00 in SSC and HSC**, with at least **3.50 in each exam**, as per the university's undergraduate admission eligibility. Confirm group-specific rules in the circular.
2. 2
   **Apply online between 11–25 November 2026** at **admission.eis.du.ac.bd** — select the A unit, fill SSC/HSC roll and registration, upload your photo and pay the application fee online before 11:59 PM on 25 November.
3. 3
   **Download your admit card** from the portal when it opens, and check your **exam date, time and seat plan** for the A unit.
4. 4
   **Sit the test** within the 5–26 December window — carry the admit card and HSC registration card to the centre.
5. 5
   **Check results on the portal**; selected candidates complete admission formalities on campus before classes start in January 2027.

## Good to know

- ✓
  DU runs admission through **several units** (A for science, B for business studies, C for humanities, plus separate units for fine arts and IBA) — each has its own test day inside the announced window.
- ✓
  The **affiliated colleges of DU** run a **separate admission process** through their own portal — their application is not the same form as the main A/B/C unit tests. Check the circular so you apply on the right portal.
- ✓
  On application time, be careful with your **roll and registration numbers** — mismatches with board data can block your admit card.
