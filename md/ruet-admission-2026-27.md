## How RUET admission works — step by step

1. 1
   **Check the prospectus** — the guideline PDF on the admission site lists eligible groups, minimum GPA requirements and the subject-wise seats (last cycle, departments such as ETE, ECE, IPE and Mechatronics each admitted around 60 students).
2. 2
   **Apply online** on admission.ruet.ac.bd when applications open — SSC/HSC details, photo, signature and fee payment.
3. 3
   **Download the admit card** on the announced date and check your centre and seat assignment.
4. 4
   **Sit the admission test** — one MCQ exam covering the science subjects decides the merit list.
5. 5
   **Check results and choose departments** on the portal; admitted students complete enrollment before classes begin.
