What happened — in simple words

The education boards published the routine for the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC), Alim and equivalent examinations of 2026 — the general boards for HSC, the Madrasah board for Alim and the Technical board for BM / Vocational (DIBS) students.

  • ✓ Written exams started on Thursday, 2 July 2026 with the Bangla 1st paper — all boards used one common schedule.
  • ✓ The written part ran for about five weeks and ended in early August (8 August 2026).
  • ✓ Practical exams for science and technical subjects were completed by 15 August 2026.
  • ✓ Every exam started at 10:00 AM (সকাল ১০:০০টা).

Exam rules students should know

  • ✓ Take your seat at least 30 minutes before the exam starts — being late means missing the paper.
  • ✓ Bring your admit card and registration card to every exam — examiners check them before you enter the hall.
  • ✓ Only a board-approved calculator is allowed where the subject permits one — mobile phones are strictly prohibited.
  • ✓ Write your roll and registration numbers correctly on the OMR and answer sheets — wrong marking affects your result processing.

Keep this in mind: the board authority can change the schedule if needed. Above summary is from public notices and board updates — always double-check your own subject dates in the official routine PDF from your board's website.
