HSC Result 2026 — when is it?#

Pass rate: 52.12% (Prothom Alo, 31 Oct 2026). 40,084 students secured GPA-5. 1,270,583 candidates across 11 boards (9 general + Madrasah + Technical). SMS number for 2026: 16140 (changed from 16222). Re-scrutiny window: 1–7 Nov 2026.

The results of the HSC, Alim and equivalent examinations of 2026 are published on Saturday, 31 October 2026 — according to the education boards' latest notice. The 2026 exams began on 2 July and the last written paper was held on 8 August 2026, with practical exams completed by 15 August.

  • ✓ Results for all 11 boards come out together on the same day.
  • ✓ Admission results usually go live around 10:00 AM in the morning.
  • ✓ With your result you can see GPA and the full marksheet with subject marks.
  • ✓ If you are not happy with any marks, there is a re-scrutiny (board challenge) window right after the result day — fees and forms are published by each board.

How to check your result — 3 easy ways#

Way 1

Online — full marksheet (recommended)#

  1. 1 · Open educationboardresults.gov.bd or eboardresults.com.
  2. 2 · Select examination: HSC/Alim/Equivalent.
  3. 3 · Select year: 2026, then select your board.
  4. 4 · Type your Exam Roll number and Registration number.
  5. 5 · Press Submit — your full marksheet opens, ready to save or print. This marksheet is accepted for university admission.
Way 2

SMS — works from any phone#

Send a message in the format below to 16140 (any mobile operator, works even without internet):

HSC BOARD ROLL 2026 → 16140

Example: HSC DHA 113456 2026 — board name as first 3 letters. Standard SMS charges apply.

Way 3

From your college#

Your college also receives the results. Visit the office after the result goes live and collect a printed copy — useful if the websites are busy on result day.

Keep this in mind: the result date above is from the boards' latest notice — final confirmation comes from your board on result day. Websites get busy shortly after 10:00 AM, so try again or use SMS. Frequently asked questions

Short answers based on the dates and instructions in this guide.

When was the HSC result 2026 published? +

The HSC, Alim and DIBS results were published on Saturday, 31 October 2026, according to the education boards' latest notice.

How can I check the HSC result 2026 online? +

Open educationboardresults.gov.bd or eboardresults.com, select HSC/Alim/Equivalent, choose 2026 and your board, then enter your roll and registration number.

What is the SMS format for the HSC result 2026? +

Send HSC <space> BOARD <space> ROLL <space> 2026 to 16140. For example: HSC DHA 113456 2026.

Which exams are covered by the HSC 2026 result? +

The result covers HSC, Alim and DIBS candidates across all 11 education boards on the same day.
