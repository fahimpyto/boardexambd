What we know so far (real-time status)

As of 23 October 2026, the MBBS/BDS admission circular for 2026-27 has not been published yet on the DGME portal. Early reports place the admission test tentatively on 4 December 2026 — that timing depends on the HSC result (scheduled for 29 October 2026), because the test is scheduled after the results come out. Expect the official circular and the form fill-up window to appear on dgme.teletalk.com.bd around mid-November 2026, matching last year's pattern.

  • ✓ Last cycle, online form fill-up ran from 11 to 21 November 2025 with an application fee of Tk 1000.
  • ✓ The test is a 100-mark MCQ paper in 1 hour 15 minutes — Biology, Chemistry, Physics, English and General Knowledge.
  • ✓ MBBS and BDS sit the same question paper on the same day — your merit decides which seats you can choose.
  • ✓ A separate, longer form window exists for foreign applicants (last cycle it stayed open until January 2026).

How to apply — step by step

  1. 1 Wait for the circular on dgme.teletalk.com.bd — expected around mid-November 2026. Check your eligibility against it: last cycle's minimum combined SSC+HSC GPA (reported as 8.50, with at least 3.50 in each) is a guide — the new circular is final.
  2. 2 Fill the online form with your SSC and HSC roll, registration, board and quota details. Pay the Tk 1000 fee through the online payment options.
  3. 3 Download your admit card from the portal and check your test centre and roll.
  4. 4 Sit the 100-mark MCQ test — reach the centre early, follow OMR rules, and watch negative-marking instructions if included in the circular.
  5. 5 Check the merit list on the portal; selected candidates choose their college and complete admission with document verification.

The 4 December date is tentative — it is reported from early updates and depends on the HSC result schedule. The official DGME circular at dgme.teletalk.com.bd is the only final source for date, eligibility, seats and rules. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site.
