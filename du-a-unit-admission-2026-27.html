What we know so far (real-time status)#

As of 23 October 2026, Dhaka University has confirmed its 2026-27 undergraduate admission schedule: online applications open at 12:00 noon on 11 November 2026 and close at 11:59 PM on 25 November 2026. Admission tests for all units are set between 5 December and 26 December 2026, and first-year classes are being planned from January 2027.

The exact day of the "A" unit (science) test inside that window is confirmed on the admission portal with the seat plan — based on previous cycles, science-unit candidates should expect the test around mid-December. Keep the portal bookmarked so you don't miss the announcement.

Confirmed: application window (11–25 Nov) and unit-wise test period (5–26 Dec) for the 2026-27 session. Watch admission.eis.du.ac.bd for the A-unit date, seat plan and admit card.

How to apply — step by step#

  1. 1 Check eligibility — for the A (science) unit the common requirement is a combined GPA of 8.00 in SSC and HSC, with at least 3.50 in each exam, as per the university's undergraduate admission eligibility. Confirm group-specific rules in the circular.
  2. 2 Apply online between 11–25 November 2026 at admission.eis.du.ac.bd — select the A unit, fill SSC/HSC roll and registration, upload your photo and pay the application fee online before 11:59 PM on 25 November.
  3. 3 Download your admit card from the portal when it opens, and check your exam date, time and seat plan for the A unit.
  4. 4 Sit the test within the 5–26 December window — carry the admit card and HSC registration card to the centre.
  5. 5 Check results on the portal; selected candidates complete admission formalities on campus before classes start in January 2027.

Good to know#

  • ✓ DU runs admission through several units (A for science, B for business studies, C for humanities, plus separate units for fine arts and IBA) — each has its own test day inside the announced window.
  • ✓ The affiliated colleges of DU run a separate admission process through their own portal — their application is not the same form as the main A/B/C unit tests. Check the circular so you apply on the right portal.
  • ✓ On application time, be careful with your roll and registration numbers — mismatches with board data can block your admit card.

Always confirm officially: the unit-wise exam schedule, seat plan, eligibility details and fee are final only in the official circular at admission.eis.du.ac.bd. BoardExamBD is an independent informational site — treat this page as a helpful summary, not the official notice.
