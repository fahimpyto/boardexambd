What happened in 2026 (real-time recap)

The HSC 2026 written exams ran from 2 July to 8 August 2026, and after the written papers finished, the boards required all practical examinations to be completed by 15 August 2026. Unlike the written tests, practicals were organized by each institution at its own centre — every college announced its own date list and the boards assigned examiners.

  • ✓ Practical exams cover the science group — Physics, Chemistry, Biology — plus papers with practical/oral parts such as ICT.
  • ✓ Each institution scheduled its practical days shift-wise and announced them on its own notice board — there is no single board routine for practical dates.
  • ✓ Practical marks count inside the subject's total — they merge with your theory marks in the final result.
  • ✓ Alim (Madrasah) and BM/DIBS (Technical) candidates follow their own boards' equivalent practical arrangements.

How practical exams work

  1. 1 Your college announces the dates — usually subject-wise and shift-wise, right after the written exams of that subject end.
  2. 2 Bring your practical notebook and lab records — examiners check them along with your experiment performance.
  3. 3 Board-appointed external examiners evaluate the experiment, viva/answer sheet and your notebook during the session.
  4. 4 The college consolidates practical marks with the theory marks and forwards them to the board for the final result.
