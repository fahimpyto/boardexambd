As of 23 October 2026, BUET has confirmed the date of its undergraduate admission test for the 2026-27 session — the preliminary test is set for 16 January 2027. The full circular (with the application window, fee and rules) is expected to be published soon — based on the last cycle, the online application period should run from around mid-November to early December 2026.

How BUET admission works — step by step

  1. 1 Apply online on the BUET admission site when the circular opens — fill your SSC and HSC details, photo and signature, and pay the application fee (Tk 1000 in the last cycle).
  2. 2 BUET prepares a shortlist from the applicant pool based on the selection criteria in the prospectus — admission is far more competitive than the number of seats.
  3. 3 Shortlisted candidates sit the preliminary MCQ test (16 January 2027).
  4. 4 Candidates advancing further take the written test a few weeks later — final merit is drawn from it.
  5. 5 Merit-list candidates choose departments and confirm seat on the portal with document verification.
